Pricing & Trust

What we found
  • ₹3,499 → ₹4,000 → ₹4,720 +35% from the website to checkout.
  • SLIM 90 pages say “Total worth ₹2,13,000”, then sell for ₹4,000. A 98% discount is hard to believe and makes the program look cheap.
  • Weight-loss promises differ by page: 10 kg, 12–15 kg, 15 kg+.
  • No FAQ, refund policy, WhatsApp or coach credentials on any of the 5 pages.
  • Testimonials praise a ₹49 plan no longer sold. Typos, and the brand spelled 3 ways.
What we’ll do
  • One final price on every page, the same as checkout
  • A believable value: show what’s included at a realistic worth, about ₹38,000–₹40,000, so ₹4,000 feels like a great deal
  • One name and one promise per program, chosen by Her Will
  • Add FAQ, WhatsApp, coach bios and current testimonials; clean the copy

The App

You have an app. It is working against you. Reviewed live on 14 October on a brand new account, which is exactly what a first-time customer sees. Everything below is on your App Store listing or inside the app right now.

What a new customer meets
The storefront prices are not believable

SLIM 90 shows ₹4,000 struck through from ₹2,13,000. A 98% discount. In India an unsubstantiated “was” price is exposure under ASCI and the consumer protection rules.

Every product image is a blank grey box

All five programmes. No photograph, no Deepa, nothing. The shop window is empty.

A new signup sees one course, then “That’s it! No more to show.”

No sample, no trial, no preview of what ₹4,000 actually buys.

What is broken or dormant
The Live tab says “Whoops! No lives found!”

You run two live sessions every day. The tab built to show them is empty.

The community has been silent since 22 September

Three weeks. 43 unread. Every message is a broadcast from the brand, no member conversation.

The App Store listing
TagMango Incis the publisher. Your brand does not appear as the seller, and nothing links back to her‑will.com.
Educationis the category. Not Health & Fitness. Nobody browsing fitness will ever find you.
2ratings in eleven months. Zero written reviews, in India or anywhere.
13 Nov 2025was the last update. The day after launch. No release notes have ever been written.
What we would do about it

Week one, free: recategorise to Health & Fitness, rewrite the listing around SLIM 90 and real outcomes, remove the strike-through prices before they become a liability, and put a real image on every programme. Then: make the first screen a customer sees sell the programme rather than explain the app, switch the Live tab on, and restart the community. None of this needs a new app built.

Three competitors, one open gap.

FitWithPari

Bangalore, women-only live online. ₹2,999 a month. 16.1K followers.
What their content does

Workouts and member medals. The medal post got 82 likes against 4 to 37 on the rest.

What we take

Recognition beats demonstrations.

Where Her Will wins

A small but engaged community. Her Will’s first job is an engaged audience, not a bigger one.

WOWFIT

Women’s home programme by Vibha Mahajan. ₹27 entry offer. 330K followers.
What their content does

Problem workouts ending “Comment LEGS”. That post: 2,299 likes and 852 comments.

What we take

A keyword ending multiplies comments.

Where Her Will wins

Its page leads with promises like “Lose 6 to 7 kg”. Her Will can be the honest alternative.

YourTrainer

Female coaches. ₹5,000 to ₹14,000 a month. 9.6K followers.
What their content does

Myth-versus-fact food and hormone carousels. 39 to 99 likes on a small account.

What we take

Food myths work at any size.

Where Her Will wins

Useful content, but a small audience and a high monthly price. Her Will costs far less over 90 days.
